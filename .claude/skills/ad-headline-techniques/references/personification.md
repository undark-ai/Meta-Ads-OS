# Personification

Give human emotions, motivations, or behaviors to the product, a competitor, or the problem being solved. You decide what to personify and what traits to assign.

**Examples:**
- Corona: "Some cans have all the fun."
- Oreo: "Milk's favorite cookie."
- Ramp: "Receipts now organize themselves."
- Columbia: "This jacket is a climate denier."
- Riverside: "Riverside records locally. Wi-fi is like 'Can I help?' And we're like, 'No, man, we got this.'"
- Ray: "Talk to your money."
- Gay Lea Spreadables: "Margarine is, like, so freaked out right now."
- Notion Mail: "The inbox that thinks like you."
- TestGorilla: "Dear Resume, I've met someone else. Sincerely, Jane (Hiring Manager)."
- Kapa: "AI that says 'I don't know'."
- Allup: "Tastes like a blueberry farted in my mouth." / "Tastes like the ghost of an orange." / "Tastes like a mango yelled at my water."
