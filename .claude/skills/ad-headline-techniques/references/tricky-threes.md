# Tricky Threes

Set up a pattern with two normal, expected items, then break the pattern with a third item that's funny, weird, or reframes everything. The first two lull the reader in. The third is the payoff.

**Structure:** "[something expected], [something expected], [surprising twist]."

**Rule:** each headline must contain exactly three comma-separated items and end immediately after the third item.

**Examples:**
- The Nature Company: "Distant galaxies, far off constellations, new neighbors across the street."
- Advertise or Die: "A good ad should make you laugh, cry... and most importantly, money."
- Postmates: "When you like your coffee without sugar, cream, or pants."
- British Airways: "Business. Leisure. Because this weather sucks."
- Bumble: "Tall, dark, and actually reads that book you recommend."
- Delivereasy: "We can deliver fish & chips to your home, to the beach, but not to the aquarium. Because they shouldn't have to see that."
- Wild Turkey: "Kentucky has produced 2 Presidents, 4 Vice-Presidents, and one distinguished liquor cabinet member."
- Denver Museum of Nature & Science: "When you travel down the Amazon you're one of three things: Fisherman, tourist, or dinner."
