# Wake-Up Call

Alert the reader that something they're doing (or ignoring) is quietly costing them. Before writing, think hard about the target audience: what habits, assumptions, or blind spots are common in their world? Name the specific behavior or gap, then let the sting of the question do the work.

**Common forms:**
- "You're still doing [outdated thing]?"
- "Is [bad thing] the only [thing] you've done about [problem]?"
- "[Thing they're ignoring] is happening. Are you paying attention?"

**Examples:**
- Fondue: "WTF! You're still using coupons?"
- IBM: "Is your best employee working somewhere else?"
- Mount Sinai: "Is airport security the only inspection your body has gone through in years?"
- Kagi: "Is your browser a snitch?"
- Mazda: "Honestly now, did you spend your youth dreaming about someday owning a Nissan or a Mitsubishi?"
- Qualli: "Your users are talking. Are you listening?"
