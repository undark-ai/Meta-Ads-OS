# Foundational audit

The short version: is this account measurable, and is it economically viable? Run it before a
full audit on an unfamiliar account, or when the user wants a verdict quickly.

## Sequence

| § | Section | Agents |
|---:|---|---|
| — | Preflight, discovery, scope | 02 |
| 1 | Business & economics — **gate** | 7–20 |
| 2 | Tracking & measurement — **gate** | 21–36 |
| 3 | Reconciliation — **mandatory** | 37–42 |
| 5 | Delivery blockers and account-level performance | 03, 04, 51–54 |
| 4 | Account structure | 43–50 |

## What it answers

1. Can these numbers be trusted? (§2 verdict)
2. Does Meta's claim survive contact with banked revenue? (§3)
3. What is break-even, and is the account above it? (§1)
4. Is anything blocking delivery right now? (§5)
5. Is the structure capable of learning? (§4)

## What it does not answer

Creative, audiences, catalog, funnel, incrementality, and where the next dollar goes. Say so
explicitly — a foundational audit that reads like a full one is worse than no audit, because the
account believes its creative has been reviewed.

Close the coverage ledger with the sections that ran, and list the rest as not attempted in this
workflow — distinct from `BLOCKED`.
