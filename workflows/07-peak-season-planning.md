# Peak season planning

For the run-up to a peak — BFCM, holiday, a launch window. Planning, not auditing: the output is
a plan with dates, budgets and pre-agreed decisions.

## Sequence

| Step | What | Skills / agents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prior-year peak: what actually happened, at what CPM, at what CAC | 148–151, `business-context` |
| 2 | Break-even at **peak CPMs**, not at today's. Peak economics are different economics | `cac-and-roas` |
| 3 | Inventory against planned spend — the constraint nobody models until it binds | `shopify-extraction` |
| 4 | Creative production plan and lead times, against the account's own creative lifespan | `creative-testing-engine`, `frequency-and-saturation` |
| 5 | Offer plan by phase, with realised-discount modelling | `meta-offer-strategy`, `offers` |
| 6 | Audience and exclusion plan; existing-customer treatment | `meta-audience-strategy` |
| 7 | Measurement readiness — fix tracking **before** the peak, never during | 21–36 |
| 8 | Budget phasing, and the pre-agreed decisions | `scale-matrix`, `14-day-change-control` |

## Three phases

| Phase | Timing | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | 4–6 weeks out | Fill retargeting pools, test creative and offers while CPMs are cheap, finish tracking work |
| **Peak** | The window | Spend against a plan, not against a dashboard. Decisions were made in advance |
| **Wind-down** | 1–2 weeks after | Capture the pool the peak built; do not cut instantly |

## The rules that peaks break

- **Do not change tracking during the peak.** An event configuration change rewrites how the
  account measures itself from that moment; doing it mid-peak destroys the comparison the peak
  exists to produce. Ship measurement work in the build phase or not at all.
- **Do not launch new campaigns into the peak.** Learning phase costs roughly a week of stable
  delivery, and a week is most of the window. Scale what is already learning-stable.
- **Do not read peak performance against non-peak benchmarks.** Peak CPMs are structurally
  higher; a CAC that would be alarming in September may be fine in November, and the break-even
  computed at peak CPMs is the one that decides.
- **Agree the decisions in advance.** What triggers more budget, what triggers a pause, what
  happens if inventory runs short. Decisions made at peak, under pressure, on incomplete data,
  are worse than decisions made calmly six weeks earlier.
- **Wind-down is a plan, not an absence.** The peak built a retargeting pool and a customer base;
  cutting spend to zero on the 27th throws both away.

## Deliverables

A dated plan: phase boundaries, budget by phase, the creative slate with production deadlines,
the offer calendar, the exclusion plan, the pre-agreed decision triggers, and the measurement
work that must be finished before the build phase ends.
