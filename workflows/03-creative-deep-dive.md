# Creative deep dive

Sections 7–11 plus 27. The creative learning system, without the rest of the audit.

Run it when the account's measurement is already known to be sound, or when the user's question
is specifically about creative.

## Why §2 still runs

Abbreviated, but it runs. A creative ranking where modelled share varies materially across ads
is partly a ranking of measurement artefacts, and the deep dive has to know whether that is the
case before presenting an angle verdict.

## Why §11 runs before §9

Relevance diagnostics tell you whether Meta is penalising an ad's delivery. An ad with poor
Quality Ranking is expensive for reasons that have nothing to do with which angle wins, and
including it in an angle comparison without that context distorts the comparison.

## Deliverables

`creative-database.csv` · `top-creatives.html` · `top-creatives-chrome.html` · the fatigue
verdict per ad · the angle learning with its confounds named and its classification source
stated · the untested angle gaps from the Ad Library · the testing-engine verdict.

Close with **what to make next** — briefs, not adjectives — and the one test that would most
sharpen the next round.
